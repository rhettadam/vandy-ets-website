About

Who we are

The Energy Transition Society is a group of students interested in learning about energy transition careers, or working in them. Members study Engineering, Economics, Philosophy, HOD, Climate Studies, and many more fields.

We hold full body meetings about twice a month to talk current events in alternative energy and policy, and to plan speaker events. We bring several speakers each semester.

Founded in spring 2024 at Vanderbilt.

Get involved

How to join

Open to any major, year, and GPA. Come to a meeting and see how it feels.

  1. 01

    Join on Anchor Link

    Follow The Energy Transition Society for meeting details and event updates.

  2. 02

    Come to a meeting

    GBMs usually run once to twice a month on Wednesday nights.

  3. 03

    Help run things

    Help plan speakers, networking nights, or career events. Apply for an officer role when the board opens applications.
